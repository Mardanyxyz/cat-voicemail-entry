Entrants write one voicemail greeting as if a cat recorded it, at most 120 characters, and submit it as plain text in their entry. A judge model reads the greetings two at a time and picks the funnier one each time, working from a judge brief published with the bounty that asks whether the line is really a cat's voicemail, whether it lands a laugh, whether the cat has a character of its own, whether the joke is specific to cats, and whether it works spoken aloud. The funniest greeting takes the whole reward.
